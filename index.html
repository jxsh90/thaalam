thaalam.

Control Ableton Live's click and tempo from your iPhone.

Thaalam is a stage remote for musicians. Switch Live's metronome on and off, nudge or ramp the tempo, tap a tempo, and jump to song tempos from a setlist. Your music director keeps full control of Live. Thaalam never starts or stops playback.

What you need

Ableton Live 10.1.13 or newer on a Mac, and the iPhone on the same Wi-Fi as the Mac.

The free Thaalam script for Live (for click on/off, confirmed tempo and ramps). Install it with the "Install Thaalam" Mac app, then in Live open Settings → Link, Tempo & MIDI and choose Thaalam as a Control Surface (Input and Output: None).

Tempo can also follow over Ableton Link without the script.

Pairing

Live shows a 6-digit pairing code at the bottom of its window until a phone pairs. In Thaalam, open Settings, tap your Mac under "Macs on this Wi-Fi", enter the code and tap Pair. You only pair once.

Troubleshooting

  • Mac not listed: allow Local Network for Thaalam (iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network), and check both devices are on the same Wi-Fi. You can also enter the Mac's IP address.
  • Cannot connect: allow incoming connections for Live in the Mac's firewall. Some guest and church networks block devices from talking to each other; use a private network or the iPhone's Personal Hotspot.
  • Too many phones: up to 8 phones can connect to one Mac at the same time.
