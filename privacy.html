Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026.

Summary

Thaalam collects no personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it sends nothing to any server run by us or anyone else.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Your setlists, songs, tempos, ramp presets and settings.
  • The address of the Mac you chose, and a pairing token kept in the iPhone's Keychain.

Deleting the app deletes this data.

Local network

Thaalam talks only to devices on your own local network: to the Thaalam script inside Ableton Live on your Mac, and to other Ableton Link apps. This is why iOS asks for Local Network permission. These connections never leave your network.

On your Mac

The Thaalam script for Ableton Live stores its pairing code and the names of paired phones in a file on your Mac. It does not send them anywhere.

Children

Thaalam does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions: portaldevsup@gmail.com
